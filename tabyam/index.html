tabyam

Your touchpad, as a tablet.

Tabyam is designed to make your laptop trackpad function like a drawing tablet, featuring absolute positioning and area mapping.

The premise of Tabyam is that wherever you put your finger in the trackpad, it instantly moves your cursor to that spot just like a drawing tablet. A useful feature I included is area mapping, which lets you choose what part of the trackpad you want to put to use. And another useful feature I added is a Tap-To-Click toggle, meaning your finger doesn't necessarily have to click when you land it on the trackpad, on by default.

The project's source code is currently a Linux special and a Windows release is on the works.

Project made with love for osu! players and for digital artists as an affordable drawing tablet alternative.
